Help & FAQ

Common questions

Why won’t the videos load?

The playthroughs are YouTube embeds, and YouTube blocks embeds inside some in-app browsers (like the one built into a chat or social app), which leaves a black box. Opening this page in a regular browser like Chrome or Safari usually fixes it. The red Watch on YouTube button on any game always works, and if the video just hasn’t loaded yet, give it a few seconds — there’s a note under the frame if it’s still stuck.

It says “playback on other websites has been disabled by the video owner” — what now?

That message comes from YouTube: whoever uploaded that particular video turned off embedding, so no website is allowed to play it. Open the game, tap Edit details, and paste a different YouTube link for the same game — from then on, that video is the one that plays here.

My game isn’t showing up in search

Try the game’s best-known title and check the spelling — remakes, subtitles and regional names can all matter. If it still won’t turn up, use the “Add as a custom game” option under the search box (or the Paste a list tab for lots of games at once). Custom games get a best-guess picture and playthrough video automatically, and you can fix anything on them with Edit details.

How do I change a game’s platform, video or description?

Click the game to open its details. The Platform dropdown at the top switches between the platforms that game was actually released on, and Edit details at the bottom lets you rewrite the description, paste a different YouTube link, or type a platform that isn’t in the list.

Something else is broken

Email me at dev@game-rating-game.com and tell me what you were doing when it went wrong — a screenshot helps a lot.
