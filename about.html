About

Game Rating Game

Make a tier list of the games that had the biggest impact on your life. What game changed your life?

Game Rating Game is a free website where you build your own list of the games you’ve played and rate them 1–4 stars through four rounds, from first impressions to your GOAT tier. It’s a recreation of Jerma985’s game rating game stream — build your own list and rate your favourite games.

Why I made it

Jerma ranked his favourite games on stream, and someone remade the website for it — but you couldn’t add your own games, only rate his list. I wanted to rate my games, so I built this version: the same rating game, but the list is yours. Search for any game, paste in a whole list at once, or import a file, and the site sorts out the platforms for you.

How it works

Round 0 — Build your list. Search for games on any platform, paste a list of names, or import a file. When your list looks right, start Round 1.

Round 1 — Rate everything. Give each game 1 to 4 stars. Games given 3 or 4 stars get a bye straight to the final round. Or blast a game off the list with the laser gun.

Round 2 — 1-star trios. Only 1-star games. Each screen shows up to 3 games — pick 1 to survive.

Round 3 — Pairs. Only 2-star games plus Round 2 survivors. Each screen shows up to 2 games — pick 1 to survive.

Round 4 — The tier list. Drag the survivors and your 3/4-star games into GOAT, S+, S, or S-, and arrange them inside each tier. That’s your final ranking — and you can export a picture of it to share.

Opening a game shows a playthrough video to remind you of it while you decide on a rating, and every game shows the cover art for the platform you played it on.

Free, no account, your list stays yours

Game Rating Game is free for everyone, and it’ll stay that way. There’s no account to make and nothing to sign up for — your list, ratings, round progress, and tiers are saved only in your own browser, never sent to a server. You can back them up any time with Export JSON, and bring them back by importing the file.

Keeping the site free is supported by small ads, which you can turn off for free any time, and by one-time tips on Ko-fi. Both help support me and help me create more projects like this one.
