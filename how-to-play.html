How to play

Game Rating Game

Rate the games that had the biggest impact on your life, one round at a time. Here’s how the whole game works.

Round 0 — Build your list

Put together the games you’ll rate: search for games on any platform, paste a list of names, or import a file. When your list looks right, start Round 1. If a game shows the wrong platform, open it and use the Platform dropdown at the top of its details to pick the right one — no need to re-add it.

Round 1 — Rate everything

Click a game for details. Give it 1 to 4 stars there. Or press the laser gun button on the side of the board: your cursor turns into the gun, and clicking any game blasts it off the list with an explosion — no need to open it. Press the gun again or hit Esc to put it away. All games given a 3 or 4 star gets a bye straight to the final round. Tap a game to watch its playthrough video if you’re not sure you remember it.

Round 2 — 1-star trios

Only 1-star games. Each screen shows up to 3 games. Pick 1 to survive — the other 2 are eliminated. Use Back and Next to review your picks.

Round 3 — Pairs

Only 2-star games plus Round 2 survivors. Each screen shows up to 2 games. Pick 1 to survive.

Round 4 — The tier list

Drag survivors of Round 3 and any 3/4-star games into GOAT, S+, S, or S-, and arrange them inside each tier. This is your final ranking.

Playthrough videos

Opening a game shows a playthrough video right in its details — here’s one to remind you of the game while you decide on a rating. You can also open it on YouTube with the red button. If it’s the wrong video, find the right one on YouTube, copy its link, and paste it under Edit details — from then on that video plays for that game.

Important: check your picks before moving on. Once you confirm the next round, earlier answers are locked.
