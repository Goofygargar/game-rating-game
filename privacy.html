Privacy Policy

Your privacy, in plain English

Last updated: November 22, 2026. This is the same Privacy Policy shown inside the game — it’s also here as its own page so it’s easy to find and link to.

Your games stay in your browser

Everything you do here — your game list, your ratings, your round progress, your tiers, and your settings — is saved only in your own browser, in a bit of storage called localStorage. None of it is ever sent to a server, and I never see it or share it with anyone. If you clear your browser’s data for this site, it’s gone for good, so use Export JSON if you want a backup copy.

Ads and cookies

The ads on this site are shown by third-party advertising partners — Google AdSense and Media.net. They may use cookies to show you ads, including personalised ads based on the websites you visit. You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time in your Google Ads Settings. You can also turn the ads on this site off completely, for free, with the button at the top of the page.

Analytics

I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit the site and which pages they look at. It doesn’t use cookies and doesn’t track you across the web — it just counts visits and page views so I can tell whether the site is growing.

Questions

Email me at dev@game-rating-game.com and I’ll get back to you.
